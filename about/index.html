About Honggao Cao

Independent research on AI evaluation, evidence, model error, human-AI performance, risk, and practical decision systems.

Present research

Honggao Cao's current research asks how AI systems and AI-enabled decisions can be evaluated before people rely on them. The work examines model error, evidence sufficiency, human-AI performance, source provenance, risk, value, and the practical management of AI-enabled decisions.

Research approach

The research begins with concrete judgment problems: what a system is intended to do, what could fail, what evidence would support reliance, who remains responsible, and what testing or monitoring should follow. Working papers, analytical frameworks, and interactive tools are used to make those questions inspectable in practice.

Intellectual continuity

Earlier research and analytical work on quantitative finance and risk, survey methodology and data quality, economics and social science, and program evaluation provide relevant experience with measurement, uncertainty, incentives, and institutional decision-making. That continuity informs the current agenda without treating every earlier publication as part of a predetermined AI research program.

Professional and academic background

Honggao Cao's background includes quantitative analytics, model validation, model risk management, quantitative finance, research, and applied evaluation. His financial-sector analytics and model-governance experience has centered on decisions where models, evidence, assumptions, controls, and institutional responsibilities all matter.
