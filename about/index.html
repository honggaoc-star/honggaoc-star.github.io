About Honggao Cao

I am an independent researcher with a background in economics, social-science research, quantitative analytics, model validation, and risk management.

Research and Questions

My current investigations address questions involving artificial intelligence, prediction and attribution, uncertainty, institutions, social systems, and human experience. They do not form a single theory. The questions sometimes cross disciplinary boundaries and call for different methods.

Research Approach

My approach is problem-driven, logic-abiding, and value-seeking. I begin with questions that seem meaningful, whether familiar or unconventional. I examine possible explanations, the assumptions supporting them, and relevant research and evidence. An initial idea may be strengthened, revised, or abandoned as the investigation develops.

The objective is to improve understanding. Some investigations lead to formal analysis or analytical frameworks; others lead to conceptual distinctions, practical methods, essays, or questions requiring further work. A useful result need not be a novel theory or an empirical discovery. It may offer a clearer way to organize a problem, connect relevant observations, or determine what remains unresolved.

Earlier Research and Experience

My earlier work includes research at the University of Michigan Survey Research Center on survey measurement, data quality, household economics, and retirement research. I hold a Ph.D. from the University of Chicago. My professional experience includes quantitative finance, model validation, and risk management.

These activities share concerns that continue to inform my research: what can be learned from observations, how assumptions affect conclusions, how uncertainty complicates judgment, and how evidence can support decisions. The earlier research record provides selected publications and supporting links.

AI in the Research Process

AI is both a subject of several investigations and a tool used for exploration, literature discovery, analytical criticism, drafting, and revision. AI assistance does not establish an argument’s validity or practical value. I direct the research and remain responsible for its evidence, judgments, and final content.
