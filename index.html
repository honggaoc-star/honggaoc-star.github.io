Independent research

Research and writing on evidence, judgment, systems, and human experience.

Honggao Cao works on AI evaluation and risk management, alongside broader questions about luck, social systems, outcomes, and life as it is lived. The work takes the form of research papers, analytical tools, and reader-facing essays.

Current Research

Four connected areas organize the current formal research.

Evaluation

AI Evaluation and Model Error

How governing references and task obligations can be used to classify hallucination and fidelity errors—and why classification must remain separate from localization and causal attribution.

Governance

AI Risk, Value, and Management

How an evolving value case and an independently assessed risk case can support continuing decisions to proceed, adapt, expand, restrict, or stop AI activities.

Reliance

Human–AI Performance and Provenance

How AI assistance changes performance, how knowledge of AI use changes evaluation, and when provenance is useful evidence rather than a coarse substitute for direct measures of quality, contribution, verification, or compliance.

Practice

Decision Systems and Practical Management

How analytical methods and operational tools can support clearer decisions without hiding assumptions, limits, or responsibility.

Manuscript

How We Get Where We Are

People, Institutions, and Luck in an Unequal World

Honggao Cao · September 2026 · 53 pages

Why can relatively modest differences in individual capabilities coexist with much larger differences in rewards, resources, and influence?

This manuscript follows how personal abilities, opportunities, resources, and relationships become consequential through institutions, and how outcomes change the conditions of later activity. Research and examples from housing, farming, health care, technology, finance, and irrigation connect present positions with the histories that produced them. The discussion also asks what counts as improvement, for whom, through which means, and over what period.
