Completed manuscript v1.1

How We Get Where We Are

People, Institutions, and Luck in an Unequal World

Honggao Cao · September 2026 · 54 pages

Why can relatively modest differences in individual capabilities coexist with much larger differences in rewards, resources, and influence?

This manuscript follows how personal abilities, opportunities, resources, and relationships become consequential through institutions, and how outcomes change the conditions of later activity. Research and examples from housing, farming, health care, technology, finance, and irrigation connect present positions with the histories that produced them. The discussion also asks what counts as improvement, for whom, through which means, and over what period.
