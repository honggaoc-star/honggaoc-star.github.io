A Second Life

Opportunity, Choice, and Luck Along the Roads Not Taken

Honggao Cao · October 2026

What can the roads not taken tell us about the life we actually lived?

What this paper helps us understand

A long career in one place may reflect repeated choices to stay—or unsuccessful attempts to leave. This paper helps readers look beyond the final outcome to the opportunities, intentions, and constraints along the way. It separates what evidence can recover about a life from what assumptions let us explore about another, without treating an imagined alternative as a known better outcome.

Abstract

Imagine living a second life: would we make the same choices, and what might have followed from opportunities we missed or never encountered? Through a long-tenured professional, a lifelong hometown resident, and a student who narrowly misses a medical-school qualification, this essay examines what a realized life can conceal about aspirations, attempted choices, constraints, and consequential events. It distinguishes historical reconstruction of what occurred from scenario analysis of what might have occurred, and considers how opportunity, choice, and luck shape both subsequent possibilities and retrospective judgments. Although alternative lives cannot be observed, revisiting the roads not taken can deepen our understanding of the life we actually lived.
